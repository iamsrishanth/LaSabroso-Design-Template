About La Sabroso

A sensory sanctuary in Madhapur where Italian elegance meets Indian spice.

The house

A self-taught kitchen with a big smile

La Sabroso opened in Madhapur in 2023, built by a self-taught BTech foodie with a love for bold, comforting food. The name means tasty in Spanish, and the menu runs from Italian pastas and thin-crust pizzas to Indian-fusion signatures and artisan coffee.

The room is boho by design: hanging lights, textured quote walls, graphic art above upholstery, and space for long conversations.
